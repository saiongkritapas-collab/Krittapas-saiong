## Business Understanding
Many shops in New Zealand are closing down or moving out. This project aims to find out why, in order to better understand the current state of the New Zealand economy.

**Question:** Why are many shops in New Zealand closing down or moving out?

**Type of analysis:** Diagnostic

**Hypothesis:** Shops are closing because of rising taxes, high inflation and high interest rates.

## Data Understanding
Data elements:
- Number of business openings and closures
- Inflation rate (CPI)
- Real and nominal GDP
- Tax rates and tax revenue
- Interest rates (Official Cash Rate)
- Consumer spending / retail sales

Data sources:
- Stats NZ (Business Demography, CPI, GDP, Retail Trade Survey)
- Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ)
- Inland Revenue (IRD)
- data.govt.nz
